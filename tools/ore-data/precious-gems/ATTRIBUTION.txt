Earth ore deposits, group "precious-gems" (decision 0065): attribution and licences
==================================================================================

What is in git here and in assets/earth/config/ore-deposits-v1/precious-gems.json is derived values only:
positions, tonnages, grades, depths, sizes and ages of named deposits, written by hand into deposits-*.csv,
areas.json and extra-evidence.json, plus the scripts that check them. No third-party table, text, map or image is
copied. Downloads used for checking stay in C:\VS mods\Earth\rawdata\ore\precious-gems\ (never in git).

Every deposit row names its source id (column "src"); the full list with URLs is sources.json and
sources-industrial.json, copied into the catalogue's sources[]. They fall into three kinds:

1. Public domain (U.S. Government works)
   - U.S. Geological Survey, Major Mineral Deposits of the World (Schulz and Briskey 2005, Open-File Report
     2005-1294), https://mrdata.usgs.gov/major-deposits/ : positions and sizes of many lode deposits (source id
     usgs-major-deposits). Five of its positions are wrong; see proposed-coordinate-corrections.csv.
   - U.S. Geological Survey, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 and 2026 (gold; gemstones), https://pubs.usgs.gov/
     periodicals/mcs2026/ : world mine production and reserves used only for validation
     (reference-mcs2026.json, made by fetch_reference.py; ids usgs-mcs-2025).
   - Berger et al. 2014, sediment-hosted gold deposits of the world (USGS) (lit-berger-2014); USGS Professional
     Paper 1802 chapters H (fluorine) and J (graphite); USGS Minerals Yearbook quartz crystal; USGS/AGS Afghanistan
     non-fuel mineral assessments (Sar-e-Sang lapis).
   - The heat-map bake's own MRDS-derived evidence (public domain, USGS Mineral Resources Data System) is not
     changed by this group; heatmap-overrides.json drops nothing.

2. Public statistics and well-known published figures, used as facts (confidence q B or C)
   Total tonnage, grade or contained metal of named deposits as published by geological surveys, company reserve and
   resource statements, and peer-reviewed literature. Figures are facts, not creative expression; only the number is
   used, and each row cites where it was published. These include World Gold Council above-ground stocks, Kimberley
   Process statistics, OECD-NEA/IAEA "Red Book", the national surveys (BGS, GEUS, CPRM, Servicio Geologico
   Colombiano, Geological Survey of India, GeoSphere Austria, Czech Geological Survey, State Geological Institute of
   Slovakia, IGME, Bayerisches LfU, Yukon Geological Survey, Montana BMG, Arkansas GS, Wyoming SGS, NMBGMR, Ontario
   GS), the company statements (Barrick, Newmont, AngloGold Ashanti, Gold Fields, Polyus, Kinross, Agnico Eagle,
   B2Gold, IAMGOLD, Centamin, Evolution, Lundin Gold, Sumitomo Metal Mining, Eldorado, OceanaGold, De Beers/Anglo
   American, ALROSA, Rio Tinto, Petra, Lucara, Gem Diamonds, Namdeb, Gemfields, Cameco, NexGen, ERA,
   Kazatomprom, Rossing, Swakop Uranium, Paladin, Orano, DIAMO, Wismut, UCIL, Syrah, Black Rock, Triton, Graphit
   Kropfmuehl, Mineral Commodities, Sibelco), and the literature listed in sources.json (Goldfarb et al. 2005,
   Frimmel 2014, Phillips and Hughes 1996, Wall et al. 2004, Deng et al. 2020, Klemm and Klemm 2013, Levtzion,
   Hughes, Themelis, Gems and Gemology papers and others).

3. Natural Earth (public domain), https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ : 1:10m admin-0 countries, used by the build
   (through the bake's CountryCheck) to check that each deposit is on land and inside its country.

Not used: Mindat, GEMDATA/DIADATA rows marked research-only, and any NC, ND or SA licensed data.
