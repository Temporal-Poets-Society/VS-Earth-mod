Sources of the iron-ferro ore catalogue (assets/earth/config/ore-deposits-v1/iron-ferro.json) and heat-map evidence
(tools/ore-data/iron-ferro/evidence.geojson). Only derived values (positions, tonnages, grades, outlines) are shipped.

Ni-Co laterite deposits of the world: database and grade and tonnage models
  Berger, V.I., Singer, D.A., Bliss, J.D., Moring, B.C., 2011, U.S. Geological Survey Open-File Report 2011-1058
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2011/1058/
  Licence: public domain (U.S. Government work); retrieved 29 September 2026 (docs/research/remaining-ores-2026-09-29/raw)

Podiform chromite deposits: database and grade and tonnage models
  Mosier, D.L., Singer, D.A., Moring, B.C., Galloway, J.P., 2012, U.S. Geological Survey Scientific Investigations Report 2012-5157
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2012/5157/
  Licence: public domain (U.S. Government work); retrieved 29 September 2026

An index to PGE-Ni-Cr deposits and occurrences in selected mineral-occurrence databases (nicrpge)
  Causey, J.D., Galloway, J.P., Zientek, M.L., 2009, U.S. Geological Survey Open-File Report 2009-1045
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2009/1045/  (used for filtered heat-map re-adds only)
  Licence: public domain (U.S. Government work); retrieved 29 September 2026

Major mineral deposits of the world
  U.S. Geological Survey Open-File Report 2005-1294 (ver. 2017)  https://mrdata.usgs.gov/major-deposits/
  Licence: public domain (U.S. Government work); retrieved 29 September 2026 (heat-map re-adds)

Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 (iron ore, manganese, chromium, nickel, titanium mineral concentrates, vanadium,
platinum-group metals, bauxite and alumina)
  U.S. Geological Survey, 2025  https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/
  Licence: public domain (U.S. Government work); retrieved 8 November 2026 (validation and reserve figures cited in entries)

Natural Earth 1:10m admin-0 countries (validation only, not shipped)
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/  Licence: public domain; retrieved 2 October 2026

Published endowment figures (authored-deposits.csv, authored-areas.json)
  Well-known figures from company annual reports and resource statements, national geological surveys and review papers,
  each named in the entry's "ref" field (for example LKAB, Vale, Rio Tinto, BHP, Kumba Iron Ore, Norilsk Nickel,
  Naldrett 2004 "Magmatic Sulfide Deposits", Cairncross and Beukes 2013, Lightfoot 2017, Indian Bureau of Mines,
  Geoscience Australia AIMR). Used as facts with citation; no tables, maps or text copied. Outlines are drawn by hand
  at about 5-10 km accuracy. Confidence q B or C. The compilation itself is offered under CC0 by the Earth project.
