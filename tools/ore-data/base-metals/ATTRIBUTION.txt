Base metals (Cu, Sn, Pb, Zn, Ag, Bi, Hg, W, Mo, Sb) for Earth decision 0065
==========================================================================

Files: assets/earth/config/ore-deposits-v1/base-metals.json (catalogue), tools/ore-data/base-metals/evidence.geojson
and heatmap-overrides.json (heat-map evidence). All values are derived: positions, tonnages, grades, ages and
names. No source tables, text or figures are redistributed.

  assessment of undiscovered copper resources: U.S. Geological Survey Scientific Investigations Report 2010-5090-Z.
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2010/5090/z/  (retrieved 2026-09-29; copy in docs/research/copper-tin-global-2026-09-29)

Named deposits (source id earth-named-2026)
-------------------------------------------
named-additions.csv, named-sn-w-mo-bi.csv and named-hg-sb-ag-cu.csv list well-known published figures (ore tonnage,
grade, place, first working) as facts, each with its citation in the citation column (carried into the catalogue's
`ref` field). Quality q B = a cited figure not re-checked against the primary report here; q C = an order of
magnitude, a figure recalled rather than read from the source, or an estimate (the `est` field names estimated
fields). Mindat and NC/ND/SA-licensed compilations were not used.
