
rivers-glo90.bin (in Earth Height Data 4/4): Earth's rivers laid onto Copernicus GLO-90 (decision 0053, test).

Sources:
- HydroRIVERS v1.0 (the river network, discharge and order). Lehner, B., Grill G. (2013): Global river hydrography and
  network routing: baseline data and new approaches to study the world's large river systems. Hydrological Processes,
  27(15): 2171-2186. Data is available at www.hydrosheds.org. Used under the HydroSHEDS license (free for scientific,
  educational and commercial use, with attribution).
- HydroLAKES v1.0 (lakes on the rivers, and reservoirs of at least 1 km² whose dams are left out). Messager, M.L.,
  Lehner, B., Grill, G., Nedeva, I., Schmitt, O. (2016): Estimating the volume and age of water stored in global lakes
  using a geo-statistical approach. Nature Communications, 7: 13603. Data is available at www.hydrosheds.org. Used under
  the HydroSHEDS license.
- Copernicus DEM GLO-90, as above (the valley floors and levels).

How the data was changed (Earth's tools/RiverSnap): starting from Earth's rivers.bin (the same reaches, discharge and
joints), each river's course was moved, within a few kilometres, onto the lowest line of the GLO-90 heights along it,
main rivers before their tributaries, and its water levels were fitted to GLO-90's floor along that course so that they
never rise downstream, held between the levels of the lakes and the sea it joins. Lake and sea points keep their places
and levels. The courses were then simplified to within 30 m (2 m in height) and compressed.
