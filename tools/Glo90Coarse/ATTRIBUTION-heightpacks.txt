Earth Height Data 1/4 to 4/4 (earthheightdata1 .. earthheightdata4): land heights for Earth

Files: earthdata/glo90c-index.bin and earthdata/glo90c-1.bin .. glo90c-4.bin. Each pack holds some of them; Earth's

How the data was changed (Earth's tools/Glo90Coarse): the 3-arc-second heights were averaged to cells 24 arc-seconds
high (about 741 m), with 150 × cos(latitude) columns per degree so that cells are about square in metres; points with
0 m (the sea in the source) were left out of the averages, and cells without land hold no height. Each average was
then moved away from the mean of the land cells beside it by 0.3 times the difference (a slight sharpening, so that the
interpolated surface follows the source more closely) and rounded to steps of 1 m below 64 m, 2 m below 256 m, 4 m
below 1,536 m and 8 m above. A second layer holds, on cells 72 arc-seconds high, the root mean square of the source
heights around the surface interpolated from these cells, in 16 logarithmic steps from 1 m to 1,024 m. Everything was
then compressed.
