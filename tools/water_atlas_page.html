Where Earth's water disagrees with the real map

Each card shows one place from the worldwide comparison: a cloud-free Sentinel-2 picture on the left, and on the right Earth's sea, lakes, rivers and exit channels over the Copernicus GLO-90 water mask, on the same 3″ grid as the heights. Mark each one so the fixes go after real problems first.

How the comparison works

The water mask is a by-product of the height map we already use: when Copernicus built GLO-90 it flagged every 90 m cell as land, ocean, lake or river (and flattened the water). Earth draws its water from other sources: GSHHG coasts, Natural Earth lakes, HydroRIVERS courses, RGI glaciers. Where the two disagree, either the source is wrong or old, or the mask is (it misses dry salt lakes, frozen lakes and floating ice).

The atlas counted each kind of disagreement on a 30″ grid, ranked the places by size and height, and walked every river and exit channel at 100 m steps to see where its bed leaves real water. The cards below are the top places of each kind outside Greenland and the high Arctic, plus the places you pointed out.

Earth layers in the maps are sampled at their own resolution (sea 30″, lakes 1′ and 2′), so stair steps along coasts are expected. Rivers are drawn as lines, not at their game width.
