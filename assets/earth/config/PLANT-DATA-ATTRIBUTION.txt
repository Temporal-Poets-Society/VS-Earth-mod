Earth regional ground plants, rules version 1 (2026-10-02)
Data file: regionalplants.json (generated by tools/bake_plant_regions.py from tools/plant-taxa.json)

Native distributions and accepted botanical names:
Govaerts R (ed.). 2026. WCVP: World Checklist of Vascular Plants.
Facilitated by the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew. https://doi.org/10.34885/egs6-cp24
Snapshot: 2026-06-04, downloaded 2026-09-29 from
https://sftp.kew.org/pub/data-repositories/WCVP/wcvp.zip
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Derived subset: accepted taxa (synonyms resolved to their accepted taxon) and their native
WGSRPD level-3 botanical countries; introduced and doubtful distribution records excluded,
regionally extinct native records retained (same rules as the regional trees).

Botanical boundaries:
The WGSRPD level-3 polygons in treeregions.json (Brummitt 2001, TDWG; GIS data by Justin Moat,
Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew). See TREE-DATA-ATTRIBUTION.txt for the full credit and changes.

Floristic regions (fallback only, for organisms without a WCVP range):
Takhtajan, A. 1986. Floristic Regions of the World. University of California Press.
The Floral Zones pack names (Cape, Caribbean, Central Australian, Circumboreal, East Asiatic,
Mediterranean, Neozeylandic) follow these regions; Earth expresses each as a set of WGSRPD
level-3 codes (tools/plant-taxa.json). That expression is Earth's own approximation.
Two North Atlantic seaweeds (Ascophyllum nodosum, Laminaria digitata) are further limited to
North Atlantic coasts following general phycological literature (e.g. AlgaeBase species pages).

Plant content:
Block codes, names and patches belong to the Floral Zones packs by their authors
(floralzonescaperegion, -caribbeanregion, -centralaustralianregion, -circumborealregion,
-cosmopolitanregion, -eastasiaticregion, -mediterraneanregion, -neozeylandicregion).
Earth only references their block codes; no models, textures, code or patch files are copied.
Reviewed releases and their SHA-256 checksums are recorded in regionalplants.json.

Earth's interpretation:
Country-scale native ranges, evaluated per chunk; climate, altitude, forest and water rules of
each pack still apply. Wild crops (the packs' crop patches) and organisms without an
identifiable range in the Cosmopolitan pack keep their climate-only rules.
