Earth regional fauna, rules version 4 (catalogue of 2026-11-17)

What the ranges mean
Living mammal ranges are PHYLACINE "present-natural" ranges: an estimate of each
species' natural range without human impact. They are counterfactual by design:
they include places the species has lost to people (for example leopards in
Scotland, tigers in Japan, dholes in Florida). They are not today's ranges and
not dated historical maps. An optional "current" profile uses PHYLACINE's
current ranges for the same mammals instead (162 rules); other rules are
identical in both profiles. The "1318" profile (decision 0054), used by worlds
placed as around 1318, removes from the present-natural range the regions each
species no longer lived in around 1318, by historical and archaeozoological
sources listed per rule (evidence1318), and never removes its current range.
Domestic animals went feral where people kept them around 1318; the domestic
dog's rule is a list of regions, the same in every profile.
Extinct taxa use documented regions or coarse boxes for their last natural
range before human-driven range loss or extinction (late Pleistocene to
Holocene); each rule states its period. PHYLACINE rasters are never used for
extinct taxa. Taxonomic splits absent from PHYLACINE (e.g. African golden wolf,
forest elephant, forest buffalo, eastern wolf) use the parent species' raster
restricted to the split taxon's documented regions.

Underlying range sources are retained per taxon in the source repository's
spatial-metadata.csv. Many PHYLACINE ranges derive from IUCN Red List range
maps (IUCN, The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species, https://www.iucnredlist.org);
PHYLACINE's current ranges in particular are based on those IUCN maps. Earth uses
only PHYLACINE's CC0 derived rasters, not IUCN spatial data or pages.
PHYLACINE's requested original and update citations are preserved above.

Changes: a subset of PHYLACINE present-natural and current rasters is converted from
142 x 360 Behrmann cells to packed occupancy bits; resolution is unchanged
(about 96 km). Aliases, subspecies and split mappings are explicit in source.

Region boundaries reuse Earth's treeregions.json. Boundary provenance and
underlying credits are in TREE-DATA-ATTRIBUTION.txt in this directory. Every
land or coastal site outside every polygon takes the nearest region (the polygons
do not follow the whole terrain coastline, e.g. half of Antarctica's).

and docs/regional-fauna-implementation.md in the Earth source repository.

Earth's historical boxes and island-group rules are approximate, and the
combination of extinct animals with today's climate is not a paleoclimate
reconstruction. Taxa without a defensible range are held from spawning.
Provider animal models, textures, sounds and behaviour remain in the original
optional mods and are not included in this derived range catalogue.
Source hashes, coverage tests and held taxa: docs/research/regional-fauna-2026-09-29/;

source repository.
