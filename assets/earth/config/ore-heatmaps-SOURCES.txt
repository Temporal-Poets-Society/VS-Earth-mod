Earth ore suitability, dataset earth-resource-evidence-2026-10-02-v3 (file format v1, evidence model v2 plus an authored best-guess layer). Built 2 October 2026.
Authored game weights and 0.5-degree rasterization; not an agency product or a measured ore probability.
Evidence tiers (major-deposit compilations > specialist deposits > prospects > occurrences), survey-density normalisation and low-weight host-rock/climate priors are game modelling choices.
Source IDs, hashes, normalization qualifications, exclusions and coordinate corrections: docs/research/ore-heatmaps-v1/bake-manifest.json in the Earth source project.

Earth project, authored best-guess districts (source id authored-best-guess-2026-10-02; authored for this project, no third-party data). Status: best-guess, authored from general knowledge, not survey data. 480 well-known mines, mining districts and basins in 94 countries where the survey layers above had no or weak evidence (including Tavan Tolgoi and Moatize coal, the Nord-Pas-de-Calais coalfield, Chu-Sarysu/Syrdarya ISR uranium and the Sicilian sulfur district), each placed inside its country by the Natural Earth check; weights 0.45-0.85 by size and confidence, always below survey deposit tiers; observed plane only. Replace with survey data when available (e.g. the USGS mineral-industry GIS compilations for Africa, Asia-Pacific, Eurasia and Latin America).

Research-only GEMDATA/DIADATA records with unresolved reuse/location qualifications, LGRB MIN4EU occurrences, BGS licensed gypsum linework, pure anhydrite/commonly dissolved gypsum units, pyrite-only "sulfur" records and placeholder coordinates are excluded from these runtime weights. Mindat and IAEA UDEPO are not used. Unknown coverage retains host-gated game background. Original research atlases still show additional material that is not shipped here.
