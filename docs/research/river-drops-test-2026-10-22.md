# River drops that follow the slope: generated-world check (2026-10-22)

This checks Earth 0.16.2's river pool layout ([decision 0024](../decisions/0024-hydrology-water-mod.md) step 1: drops
follow the real slope) in chunks generated by a dedicated server. The same places were generated with 0.16.1, which has
the old 1-block steps.

Nothing in Earth was changed, no save was opened, and nothing was installed. The worlds are new and live in the
git-ignored `testruns/drops-20261022` (0.16.2) and `testruns/drops-20261022-old` (0.16.1).

**Verdict.** The drops come out as planned:
- 1-block steps on the gentle Daugava;
- 2-block drops in the Rhine gorge;
- 2- and 3-block rapids on the Congo;
- Iguazu's 5-block fall.

Every drop the new layout makes is filled from the lower pool up to the upper level: a ramp of flowing water for a
1-block step, falling water plus a ramp of rapid water for 2+. No still water stands next to open air (one cell per
site, the same in both worlds). Water next to open air in general is unchanged from 0.16.1. It is the downstream tips of
ramps and the open faces of curtains.

There was one small new defect in 0.16.2, now fixed in 0.16.3 (see "The bank fix" below). A few bank columns right
beside 2+ drops were 1–2 blocks lower than the water next to them:
- Rhine gorge: 2 of 33;
- Congo: 8 of 81;
- 0.16.1: 1 at the Congo.

That water could spill sideways when a block update reached it.

## Method

| World | Mods | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **new** | Earth 0.16.2 (Debug build of `0f91ad4`) + Cylinder World 0.11.12 | under test |
| **old** | Earth 0.16.1 (`release/earthmap-0.16.1.zip`) + Cylinder World 0.11.12 | the old 1-block steps |
| **fix** | Earth 0.16.3 (Debug build, the bank fix) + Cylinder World 0.11.12 | the fix (`testruns/drops-20261022-fix`) |

Both worlds use 256000 × 256 × 102400, seed 386217, and the Earth tab's defaults. The test-only `slabaudit` helper
from the [Terrain Slabs test](terrain-slabs-test-2026-10-22.md) dumped every block of 5 × 5 chunk columns (7 × 7 on the
Congo) around five spots.

The spots were picked offline with the new `drops` mode of `tests/EarthMapTests`, which lists river drops and their
heights near a place.

| Site | What it is |
|---|---|
| `daugava_step1` (56.610, 25.345) | gentle river: 1-block steps (no 2+ drops on the Daugava at this height) |
| `rhine_gorge_drop2` (50.320, 7.531) | the Rhine gorge's one 2-block drop |
| `congo_drop3` (−5.543, 13.605) | Livingstone rapids: a 3-block drop with 2-block drops around it |
| `iguazu_falls` (−25.676, −54.433) | the listed waterfall (5 blocks at this height) |
| `iguazu_drop3` (−25.607, −54.600) | a 3-block drop upstream on the Iguazu |

[analyze.py](river-drops-test-2026-10-22/analyze.py) works as follows:
- **The river** is the connected surface water at each site: water above the highest hard block, levels within 8
  blocks of each other.
- **A column's level** is its highest still (source) water.
- **Drops** are level differences between neighbouring river columns. For each one it records what fills the lower
  column from its level up to the upper one.
- **Banks** are dry columns beside the river, measured against the highest water next to them.
- **Water next to open air** is any water cell with an empty neighbour.

Results are in [summary.json](river-drops-test-2026-10-22/summary.json), with a top view per site and world (`drops-<site>-<old|new|fix>.png`):
- blue is the pools, lighter is higher;
- green is rapid-water ramps;
- white is falling water;
- red is the lower side of 2+ drops;
- yellow is water next to open air.

To reproduce:
1. Build Earth (Debug) and `slabaudit`.
2. Run `mkrun.py <absolute run dir> <port> 256 [Earth mod folder]` once per world.
3. Run `analyze.py <out dir> old=<old run> new=<new run> fix=<fix run>`.

## The bank fix (0.16.3)

`TerrainModel.SettleSteps` now takes `curtainBanks`, which world generation passes for worlds with pool layout 1. Dry
land next to a column with a waterfall's curtain is then raised to the curtain's top, which is the level of the water
above it. Before, it was raised only to the column's own water, plus one block beside a ramp. Worlds with pool layout
0 are unchanged.

The same places generated again with the fix:

| Site | Banks near 2+ drops (0.16.2) | Banks near 2+ drops (0.16.3) | Water next to open air (0.16.2 → 0.16.3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhine gorge | −1: 2, 0: 30, +1: 1 | 0: 32, +1: 1 | 318 → 319 |
| Congo | −2: 2, −1: 6, 0: 73 | 0: 81 | 1 095 → 1 089 |
| Iguazu Falls | 0: 16, +1: 1 | 0: 16, +1: 1 | 249 → 249 |
| Iguazu 3-block | 0: 22, +3: 3 | 0: 22, +3: 3 | 298 → 298 |
| Daugava | none | none | 83 → 83 |

No bank beside a 2+ drop is lower than the water any more, and the drops are the same. The offline suite (384-high,
`tests/EarthMapTests`) gives the same river checks as with 0.16.2: no water walls, all 14 waterfalls pass, and the same
audit. The old layout (`POOLS=0`) is still identical to the baseline.

## Pool layout 2 (0.16.4): chutes, lips and plunge pools

The user saw 0.16.3 in game. The Congo's drops looked like small waterfalls, the river stayed 7 blocks deep right to
Victoria's lip, and Niagara's curtain stood next to a thin flowing layer. Decision 0024 section 1b followed:
- **Rapids** (drops of 2–4 blocks) become shallow, rocky chutes of 1-block steps.
- **Waterfalls** (5+ blocks, or listed) keep their curtain, with a 2-deep lip above and a plunge pool below.

The steps are 3 blocks apart. With 2 blocks, step lines met in bends and made small 2-block curtains:
- server, Congo: 216 pairs with 2 blocks, 147 with 3;
- offline, Rhine Loreley: 83 with 2 blocks, 44 with 3.

Rock breaking the surface stands a block above the water. With its top at the water level, the water just upstream
(a block higher) stood against air beside it: 41 still-water cells on the Congo, 11 in the Rhine gorge. With the rock
a block higher it is 1 and 0.

Generated on a dedicated server, 384 high, seed 386217 (`testruns/layout2b-20261022`, sites in
[sites-layout2.json](river-drops-test-2026-10-22/sites-layout2.json)). Depths come from
[depths.py](river-drops-test-2026-10-22/depths.py), the other numbers from
[summary-layout2.json](river-drops-test-2026-10-22/summary-layout2.json); top views are `layout2-<site>.png`.

| Site | Rapid-water columns, depth 1–2 / all | Lip above a curtain, depth | Plunge pool below a curtain, deepest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhine gorge | 252 / 316 | (the 2–4 block drops here are lake-edge drops, as in 0.16.1) | — |
| Congo (Livingstone) | 2 559 / 4 727 (the rest: rapid ramps in the plunge pools of its 6-block falls) | 1–2 at most columns | 12 (9 + 3) |
| Victoria Falls (22 blocks) | — | **1–2** (was 7) | **15** (7 + 8) |
| Niagara Falls (11 blocks) | — | not measured (the script found no upper water) | **13** (8 + 5) |

- Still water next to open air is 0–1 cell per site.
- All 14 listed waterfalls pass offline.
- No water walls or bare steps at the checked places, except 3 bare steps on the Congo, next to rocks.
- Offline (384-high standard world, all reaches), against pool layout 1:

| | blocked | uphill | unintended 2+ falls | 2+ drops at the layout's own places |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pool layout 1 (0.16.3) | 101 | 82 | 123 | 10 173 |
| pool layout 2 (0.16.4) | 101 | 87 | 119 | 3 916 (curtains of 5+ and at lakes; 2–4 block drops are chutes now) |

The 5 more uphill spots are 1-block blips on small rivers (about 100 m³/s). When a column's position along the river
is blended near a step, it lands on the other side of it. They are the same kind as the 82 before. `POOLS=1` reproduces
0.16.3's audit and suite exactly.

Without Hydrology 0.1.1, a touched chute step turns into an ordinary ramp (Hydrology 0.1.0's H3). The chute rule is
being added there (0024 1b).

**Correction (2026-10-23).** The offline numbers above for pool layout 2 came from a run with chute steps 2 apart, before
the change to 3. The committed 0.16.4 (steps 3 apart) gives 101 blocked, **93 uphill, 125 unintended 2+ falls, 4 016
2+ drops at the layout's own places**. It also fails one waterfall check: at Kaieteur, a forced 9-block drop lands just
above the lip and makes the curtain 47 blocks instead of 38. Worlds made with 0.16.4 have that. Pool layout 3 fixes it
(below). Measured from a build of commit `d2c0546`.

## Pool layout 3 (0.16.5): no rocks, the bed eased in and out

The user played 0.16.4. The chutes looked better without the rocks, which also came out as soil. And there were
"deep basins before rapid shallow walls": the bed jumped from the river's depth (9 blocks on the Congo) straight to the
chute's 2. Pool layout 3, new worlds only:
- No rocks in the chutes.
- The bed eases over 8 blocks:
  - from the river's depth up to the chute's 2 blocks before its first step, and back down after its last;
  - into the 2-deep lip over the 8 blocks before it;
  - out of the plunge pool below a curtain.
- The easing looks for chutes and curtains across reach ends, through short reaches in between, up to 32 blocks away.
  At first it looked only within the reach, and walls stayed where a chute began in the next reach but one.
- The shoal and the plunge pool also apply where a river crosses a lake at its own level.
- Above a listed waterfall, forced drops wait for the lip as well (the Kaieteur fix above). The fall takes what is still
  needed there.

Same server places and seed as layout 2 (`testruns/layout3-20261022`). The measure is bed jumps of 4 or more blocks
between neighbouring river columns at the same water level, from [depths.py](river-drops-test-2026-10-22/depths.py):

| Site | Layout 2 (0.16.4) | Layout 3 (0.16.5) |
|---|--:|--:|
| Rhine gorge | 60 | **0** |
| Niagara Falls | 89 | **24** |
| Victoria Falls | 84 | **58** |
| Congo (Livingstone) | 304 (261 of them 7 blocks) | 291 (120 of 7 blocks) |

Most of what stays on the Congo is an older seam. Where Earth's lake data lies beside the river, the lake keeps its own
shallow bed next to the dredged river channel. This is not from the drops, and it is the same in pool layout 1.

The depths are as in layout 2:
- chutes 1–2 deep;
- Victoria's lip 1–2 and its plunge pool 15;
- Niagara's plunge pool 13.

Offline (384 high, all reaches):
- the audit is the same as 0.16.4 (101 / 93 / 125 / 4 016), since the water levels don't change;
- all 14 listed waterfalls pass, Kaieteur too;
- no water walls or bare steps at the 12 checked places, the Congo included;
- `POOLS=2` reproduces 0.16.4's audit and suite exactly.
