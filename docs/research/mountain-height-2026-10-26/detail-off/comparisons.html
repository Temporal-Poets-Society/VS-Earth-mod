Earth mountain terrain comparisons

2 fresh worlds, 14 locations in each, heights 384 and 512. The original server surface maps are embedded unchanged so this file works offline.

These are overhead surface maps, not first-person skyline previews. Each map covers 96 × 96 game columns. Percentages describe the full footprint, including lower slopes; they do not measure a summit's exact treeline. The user still needs to judge mountain silhouettes and travel in game.

Compare geometry first, then ecology and rock exposure independently. Case K is a combined experiment, not a selected default: its physical-slope rule retains much more soil on several mountains and turns much St Helena rock into sand.
