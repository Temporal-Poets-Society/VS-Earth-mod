Earth mineral evidence atlas

Research snapshot: 29 September 2026. These 40 maps show imported evidence, not ore abundance or spawn probabilities. Fixed-strength 1° presence cells avoid counting dense survey records as stronger geology. Uncoloured areas are unassessed, not barren. Element maps do not establish mineral species, grade or early accessibility.

Sources: USGS MRDS, specialist global mineral compilations, potash assessment and former-Soviet coal study. Source records overlap; no global unique-deposit count is claimed. See research report and coverage audit. The separate evaporite potential map shows expert tracts, not observed sites or accessible halite.
