# Progression

What's easier or harder than in vanilla on Earth, and where to look for what you need.

With the defaults (vanilla rock layers and vanilla ores), the materials you need are where vanilla would put them, and
progression is vanilla's. What changes is the world around you: real climate, real distances, and plants and animals
by region. With Earth's own rocks or ore maps, materials lie where the real geology puts them, and Earth adds recipes so
that no region is shut out.

## The world itself

- **Climate is real.** Siberia is very cold in winter, the Sahara is hot and dry, the tropics have no winter but wet and
  dry seasons, and mountains are colder the higher you go. Where you start decides your first year more than anything
  else. See [Climate and seasons](Climate-and-seasons).
- **Distances are real.** The world is much bigger than a vanilla world: at 1:100 London to Paris is about 4,000
  blocks. Pick a scale that suits how far you want to travel. Rivers and coasts are the fast roads: boats on calm rivers
  and along the coast.
- **Seeds and animals are regional.** A wild crop grows only where it grew around 1318, so a European start finds wheat,
  flax and turnips but no maize, potatoes or cassava. With **Nature · Chickens everywhere** off, the Americas and
  Australia have no chickens to give feathers. See [Plants and animals](Plants-and-animals).
- **Soil in dry lands.** Semi-arid land, the Mediterranean and the steppes keep farmable soil instead of turning to sand.
  True deserts are sand and gravel.

## Flint

**Vanilla:** loose flint lies on the ground over flint deposits in sedimentary rock, and panning sand or gravel can
give flint.

**Earth:** with ground columns, flint lies loose where it really does: in the till, outwash, river gravel and beach
shingle of the lands the ice sheets covered, and chert weathers out of limestone almost
everywhere. Panning sand and gravel gives flint as in vanilla, everywhere.

## Refractory bricks without bauxite

**Vanilla:** refractory bricks, which you need for the coke oven and the cementation furnace, take crushed bauxite at every tier. Vanilla places bauxite in many places.

**Earth:** with **ground columns** or **Earth ore maps**, bauxite lies only where it really forms: in tropical laterite
and in the red terra rossa on warm limestone around the Mediterranean. Most of the world has none. So those worlds get
extra recipes, with real historical substitutes:

| Recipe | Ingredients | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| Grog | Hammer + any fired brick | 2 grog |
| Fire clay | 8 blue or red clay + 1 grog | 8 fire clay |
| Fire clay | 8 blue or red clay + 1 crushed quartz | 8 fire clay |
| Fireclay brick | 3 fire clay + 1 crushed quartz + 1 grog | 1 raw refractory brick (tier 1) |
| Crushed kyanite, sillimanite or andalusite | Hammer + the mineral | 1 crushed mineral |
| Refractory bricks, tiers 1–3 | Vanilla's recipes with crushed kyanite, sillimanite or andalusite, or corundum, in bauxite's place | As vanilla |

- The **fireclay brick** is the medieval answer: plain clay tempered with grog (crushed fired brick). It costs more fire
  clay than vanilla's brick, so bauxite stays the better choice where you have it.
- **Kyanite, sillimanite and andalusite** (ground-column worlds) lie in metamorphic rock (schist, gneiss, quartzite,
  slate, phyllite), in the belts where that rock is: the Scottish Highlands, the Alps, the Scandinavian mountains, the
  Appalachians and the old continental shields. They need a copper pickaxe. Loose pieces on the surface show where a deposit is.
- Tiers 2 and 3 still need bauxite, one of those minerals or corundum, besides vanilla's other ingredients.
- Vanilla's own recipes, bauxite's included, work in every world.

In worlds with vanilla rock layers and vanilla ores, the extra recipes aren't there: bauxite is where vanilla puts it.

## Volcanic-ash mortar

In ground-column worlds, **tephra** (volcanic ash) lies around volcanoes and on volcanic soils. In a barrel, 1 litre of
slaked lime and 1 tephra make 6 mortar instead of vanilla's 4: the pozzolana of Roman mortar.

## Fire clay from the ground

With ground columns, natural fire clay (kaolin) lies in the deep weathered ground of the tropics (saprolite and
laterite) and under coal seams.

## Ores

- **Vanilla ores** (the default): vanilla's deposits, plus Earth's copper, lead, zinc and platinum ores and gems. See
  [Rocks, soil and ores](Rocks-soil-and-ores).
- **Earth ore maps (experimental):** ores lie in their real districts. Copper and tin are where they were really
  mined, so a region without them means a journey. This choice is being reworked.

## Where to look

- `/earth` and its sub-commands tell you about the ground, ores, trees and animals where you stand. See
  [Commands](Commands).
- A real atlas or Google Maps works: mountains, rivers and coasts are where they are on Earth.
