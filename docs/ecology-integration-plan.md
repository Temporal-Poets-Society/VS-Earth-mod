**Earth ecology: Floral Zones and Fauna of the Stone Age**

Assessment date: 28 September 2026. Earth source: local 0.8.0, targeting Vintage Story 1.22. Scope agreed with the user: compatibility report and integration plan first; historical native ranges, including extinct species. No gameplay code, installed mod set, or savegame was changed.

29 September clarification: extinct animals should have an include/exclude setting, independent of historical native ranges. The broader [regional-world architecture proposal](regional-world-architecture.md) covers provider independence, geology, seasonal trees and the freshwater-cypress versus coastal-mangrove distinction. Its configuration examples are proposed, not implemented.

**Recommendation.** Use these two families as Earth's main content providers. Add an optional ecology component to Earth that decides **where a species can occur**, while retaining the provider's models, recipes, AI, reproduction and ordinary spawning machinery. This is feasible from the inspected source and assets, but the combination is **not yet verified in game**. Installation alone will provide more species; it will not produce the requested geographic distribution.

The survey found **8 Floral Zones packs and 20 Fauna packs**. All 28 archives contain Biomes compatibility metadata. That is a useful starting catalogue, not a complete native-range dataset. The author is still building both series, so their intended worldwide coverage exceeds their released coverage. [Floral Zones catalogue](https://mods.vintagestory.at/list/mod?a=2ABE1C9265B6B2CFEFAE&text=floral+zones), [Fauna catalogue](https://mods.vintagestory.at/list/mod?a=2ABE1C9265B6B2CFEFAE&text=fauna+of+the+stone+age).

**What Earth already supplies.** `EarthGeometry.ToGeo(x, z)` converts a spawn position into real latitude/longitude, accounting for Cylinder World's ring, the warped Pacific seam and inserted ocean gutter. It also identifies polar filler. All ecology lookups should use this transform; raw world X/Z fractions will produce the wrong geography. `TerrainModel.Column` already distinguishes sea, freshwater lake, salt lake and river, and supplies terrain/water height. Real annual/seasonal climate and forest potential are available. [Earth geometry](<C:/VS mods/Earth/src/Core/EarthGeometry.cs>), [terrain model](<C:/VS mods/Earth/src/Core/TerrainModel.cs>).

Missing pieces include a native-range layer, named river-basin membership, marine regions, coastal habitat classification and species-aware spawn filtering. `RiverReach` contains downstream connectivity but no persistent named basin ID. The existing lake/sea distinction is valuable: a salt lake must never qualify as open ocean simply because its blocks are saltwater. [River data structures](<C:/VS mods/Earth/src/Core/RiverNetwork.cs>).

**Biomes: useful reference, not a drop-in geographic solution.** Its current source divides the world by hemisphere and horizontal world fraction. Those regions do not follow Earth's continents, islands or its warped seam. A generic realm cannot distinguish the Yangtze from other East Asian rivers, nor New Zealand's individual islands. The current public API registers species and reads biome data; the inspected realm-generation interface is internal, with the factory selecting the built-in generators. A supported Earth provider would require an upstream extension or a version-specific adapter. [Realm layout](https://github.com/spooq/biomes/blob/f3ff9a58f3a00a20a85c5070b9c2b32cfd10b4ce/Biomes/src/RealmGen/BlendedRealmGen.cs), [generator interface](https://github.com/spooq/biomes/blob/f3ff9a58f3a00a20a85c5070b9c2b32cfd10b4ce/Biomes/src/RealmGen/IRealmGen.cs), [public registry](https://github.com/spooq/biomes/blob/f3ff9a58f3a00a20a85c5070b9c2b32cfd10b4ce/Biomes/src/Api/ExternalRegistry.cs).

Its river handling also specifically checks the separate Rivers mod and `flowVectors` data. Without that mod it permits both river/non-river categories; Earth's river geometry is not thereby classified. Its broad species metadata is therefore a starting point, not evidence that native habitats are already enforced. **Geographic rules.** The intended decision is:

`natural spawn allowed = historical native range ∩ suitable Earth habitat ∩ applicable provider spawn conditions`

Geography is a necessary condition, not a command to place an animal regardless of terrain or climate. Provider restrictions stay in effect unless a specific mismatch with Earth's scale has been demonstrated and corrected. For plants the same principle applies to natural generation, not player planting.

Use a layered dataset: broad terrestrial ecoregions; species-specific polygons/masks; named islands; freshwater basins; and separate coastal/pelagic marine regions. Preserve hard ocean/island barriers. Blend only legitimate terrestrial transition zones; adding randomness around a boundary must not send endemic species onto a neighboring continent. Use categorical sampling for region IDs, with higher-resolution overrides for small islands and river/coastal corridors.

For the user's historical-range choice, the default should be a **composite historical-native world on Earth's present terrain and climate**. Extinct species are included where their documented historical ranges and current habitat overlap. This is not a reconstruction of one prehistoric year: modern coastlines, ice sheets and climate do not match every species' era. Keep range provenance and confidence per species; do not silently substitute modern remnant populations or modern introductions for historical native habitat.

Expose `includeExtinctAnimals` as a separate setting, initially enabled for this chosen profile. Disabling it prevents new natural spawns of reviewed extinct taxa without changing the historical ranges of living species or deleting existing animals. Uncertain extinction status needs an explicit taxon classification/override. Reweight eligible populations within the existing regional density budget.

Crop cultivars and domestic animals need explicit treatment. Keep them available through player cultivation/domestication and the provider's established acquisition routes. Do not invent “wild native” ranges for domestic variants. Where a pack intentionally generates collectible wild crops, retain that gameplay only through a documented origin-region rule or an explicit user option.

**Planned implementation.** The names below describe proposed components; they have not been added to the codebase.

1. **`EarthEcology` location service.** Resolve real coordinates through `EarthGeometry`, then region/island/basin/marine membership and local habitat. Reuse Earth's sea/lake/river/elevation information. Add coast distance, suitable water volume and habitat labels where needed. Keep land climate and future sea-surface temperature separate; existing land-air temperature is not a validated ocean-temperature model.
2. **Species registry and optional adapters.** Detect installed supported packs. Match actual resolved entity, block and generator identifiers, including adult/baby/sex/old-tree/shrub variants. Flora generators can reside in the `game` domain even when supplied by Floral Zones, so a domain-prefix check is insufficient. Store scientific name, native mask, habitat, historical status, provenance, overrides and supported provider versions in editable data.
3. **Use existing metadata conservatively.** Read Floral Zones' legacy tree/block-patch mappings from installed assets. Fauna's conditional Biomes patches can inform a build-time audit, but do not activate arbitrary third-party patch files or add a fake `biomes` mod ID. Refine broad labels into reviewed Earth ranges. Record unmatched codes after provider updates.
4. **Natural generation and spawning gates.** Filter all relevant vegetation paths and both animal spawning paths before placement. Preserve upstream AI, breeding, drops and density caps. Geographic filtering must not kill transported animals, prevent domestication births or remove cultivated plants. Test whether companion spawning bypasses the main gate, and add a natural-spawn-specific check where needed.
5. **Earth-specific compatibility corrections.** Make the mangrove change; audit altitude/rainfall compatibility; preserve salt/freshwater distinctions. Let Earth reweight the surviving native tree candidates so vanilla species do not overwhelm regional flora, without globally weakening habitat requirements.
6. **Diagnostics and save policy.** Add proposed `/earth ecology` and species-explanation commands showing region, waterbody, matched rule and rejection reason. Save the ecology profile/data version; invalidate caches when it changes. For managed natural species, a strict profile should reject and clearly report missing mappings. Leave unrelated mods, story entities and non-natural creation paths alone.

A compact offline-baked region layer and cached, immutable rule tables should keep repeated spawn checks inexpensive. Do not query web services at runtime. Cache keys must include world/profile identity, and final checks near coasts and borders must use the candidate position. Multithreaded worldgen/runtime spawning must not temporarily mutate shared global arrays. Reuse or refactor Earth's current tree selector rather than stacking competing replacement prefixes.

New worlds are the cleanest rollout. In an existing Earth save, new flora appears in newly generated areas; runtime fauna can follow new rules, but existing animals and plants remain. Make that migration behavior explicit. Removing a flora provider leaves its blocks missing, so a tested pack/version set should accompany a long-lived save. [Floral Zones existing-world guidance](https://mods.vintagestory.at/floralzonesmediterraneanregion).

**Magelan and marine life.** These packs can make voyages richer, but they do not currently supply the requested whale/open-ocean-dolphin roster. The reviewed released catalogue has Sirenia, Chelonioidea, Spheniscidae and Iniidae; **Iniidae contains only the freshwater Baiji**. Its release archive confirms the species and freshwater block filter. Baiji geography should follow its Yangtze habitat, not general ocean temperature. [Iniidae description](https://mods.vintagestory.at/iniidae), [NOAA Baiji review](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/resource/document/chinese-river-dolphin-5-year-review-2025).

Sirenia is the first candidate for marine-hunting experiments: its released animals already supply meat/fat and aquatic behavior. Dugongs, manatees and Steller's sea cows still need appropriate coastal or freshwater habitat; they should not be scattered across the deep ocean as substitute whales. Sea turtles and penguins add further regional coastal life. Whale and oceanic-dolphin content remains a separate provider/content task. [Sirenia](https://mods.vintagestory.at/sirenia), [Chelonioidea](https://mods.vintagestory.at/chelonioidea), [Spheniscidae](https://mods.vintagestory.at/spheniscidae).

Design the marine interface now around waterbody kind, ocean basin/province, distance to coast, ecological depth and actual block clearance. Saltwater alone is insufficient because Earth has inland salt lakes. Likewise, a real 4,000 m ocean floor becomes only tens of blocks below the surface: use real bathymetry for habitat classification and generated depth for animal fit. Avoid natural spawns inside the artificial seam gutter until a deliberate rule exists.

For future Magelan features, Earth should supply habitat and natural population eligibility; Magelan should handle ship interaction, hunting tools, retrieval and storage. Keep animal behavior in the provider where practical. Later tests should include swimming near the hull, projectile/hit detection from a moving deck, carcass retrieval, unloading/reloading across voyages, population caps, and Cylinder World seam crossing. None of these interactions was exercised in this assessment.

| Stage | Deliverable | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Content baseline | Pin game 1.22.7, Earth, optional Cylinder World, then the eight flora and twelve non-provisional fauna releases. | Server/client startup, assets/recipes/handbook checks, natural spawn observation and log review. | 5 — Voyage integration | Ocean provider API and Magelan hunting experiments. | Existing aquatic content behaves correctly around moving ships; whale/dolphin support is tested only once suitable content exists. |

Representative geography checks should include the Upper Rhine/Baltics, Mediterranean Europe versus California, Cape versus Mediterranean Europe, East Asia versus North America, Australia/Tasmania/New Guinea, New Zealand/Chatham/Lord Howe, and the Yangtze versus other rivers. These compare native eligibility while holding similar climate or habitat constant where possible.

For a rhino test, verify that African species cannot spawn on another continent merely because it is warm, and woolly-rhino eligibility does not become a generic worldwide cold-biome rule. For island taxa, test both the correct island and adjacent mainland. For mangroves, test suitable tropical coasts, freshwater swamp trees, deep offshore water and inland salt lakes separately.

Run coordinate/range tests at multiple Earth sizes, with and without Cylinder World, including both sides of the date line and polar filler. Run deterministic eligibility tests as well as statistical spawn tests: zero observed animals in a short visit can mean rarity, not a range-filter failure. Record attempted/allowed/rejected spawns and rejection reasons. Measure generation time, spawn-check cost and live populations against the same world/seed baseline before tuning abundance.

**Boundaries of this assessment.** There is enough evidence to proceed with an Earth-owned ecology implementation, and enough existing content to make regions visibly different without creating new models. There is not yet a reviewed historical polygon for every species, a measured density profile, a combined playtest, or a whale provider. Those are explicit implementation and validation tasks, rather than assumed compatibility.

Keep provider archives separate from Earth's release. The mod pages mark the content All Rights Reserved; distributing Earth adapters and install references avoids bundling the authors' artwork/code into Earth. Local research downloads are evidence/cache only. Any copied Biomes source would also need its accompanying license and attribution; the recommended design does not require copying it.
