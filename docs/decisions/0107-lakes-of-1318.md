# 0107: The lakes of 1318 (decision 0064 step 3): drained lakes back, the Aral at its 14th-century low, the Salton Sea dry

Status: **in Earth 2.2.0** (21 November 2026), built on `claude/lakes-1318` (EA-0124, from `claude/terrain-2.2` at 2.2.0-rc.14), first in test
build 2.2.0-rc.16. Checked offline (probe, tests); not seen in game. New worlds only: works rules 6 (`EarthParams.Works1318`,
106) and river ends rules 3 (`EarthParams.RiverEnds`, 57); no new save field. Date: 2026-11-21.

## What the author asked

Testing 2.2.0-rc.13 (21 November 2026): "lake texcoco is missing, did we fix Aral sea in new v2 version?" After the check
and sizing (EA-0124): "Lake restoration according your recommendations and data available", "Why so long estimates?
Should be quite quick", and Tulare Lake. Decision 0064 (agreed 8 November) already had the rule: human works after 1318
undone, natural change kept; its step 3 listed the drained lakes, the Aral at about 29 m and the Bitter Lakes dry. How it
is built is Claude's, as maintainer (D72).

## What rc.13 had

(EA-0124's probe, `C:\VS mods\work\ea-0124\README.md`.) Decision 0005's revived Aral was whole at its 1960 level (53 m,
68,300 km²), one water level, rivers reaching it, but drawn on the 2′ grid: cell-shaped shores on its flat sides and a
1–3 km beach under the Ustyurt cliff. Texcoco, Fucino, Copais and the Fens' meres were missing; the Bitter Lakes were

## Decision

**Works rules 6** (`Works1318Data.LakeRules`): new worlds read `earthdata/works-1318-4.bin` (file version 5,
`tools/bake_lakes_1318.py`): works-1318-3.bin's areas byte for byte and in their order in every tile, then the areas of
`tools/lakes-1318.json`. A LAKE area is a lake at its level wherever it lies (today's lakes, reservoirs and fishponds
inside it go, as with the Dutch lakes of step 1); a LAND area is dry land over today's water.

- **Outlines.** Where the ground has barely moved since drainage, GLO-90 contoured at the level from a seed within a box
  (Fucino's fill gives 131 km² against a published 140–155; the shore sits where GLO-90 meets the level, so the lake
  never stands above the land beside it). Where it has sunk (Mexico City by more than 9 m, the Fens about 4 m, Hula 3 m)
  or GLO-90 shows trees and buildings, OpenHistoricalMap's (CC0) or OpenStreetMap's (ODbL) outline at the recorded
  level. Levels and sources per lake are in `tools/lakes-1318.json` and the research note
  (`docs/research/lakes-1318-2026-11/`).
- **Levels where the record and today's ground disagree** (the ground sank, or the lake rose and fell): the level that
  gives the recorded area (Karla 48 m for about 130 km², recorded 50.1 m in 1921; Amik 79 m for about 150 km²). At 1:10
  a block is about 10 m near sea level, so the level picks the extent, not the height. Zumpango is drawn at Xaltocan's
  level where they meet (it stood a few metres higher: a block at 1:10).
- **Shores** (`TerrainModel.ShoreOf1318`): land beside a LAKE area is held at its water top as beside the water mask's
  lakes (`OutlineShore`), so a lake over sunken ground stands in its own shores; a lake of today right beside it
  (fishponds in the drained bed, Zumpango's regulated lagoon) gives way within `LakeOutlineLayer.HoldReach` to a strip of
  shore at the higher of the two tops.
- **Rivers** (`RiverEnds.Prepare`'s lakeOf1318): a course's vertices inside a lake of 1318 are at its level, as in 0005's
  terminal lakes, the river above never lower and below never higher; the level is the one whose river water top
  (`RiverWaterTop`, RiverRelief of the relief) is the lake's (`TerrainModel.RiverLevelOfLake`). Their courses were
  levelled on today's drained ground and met the water a block or two below it.

**River ends rules 3** (`RiverEnds.Lakes1318`): new worlds read `earthdata/lakes-2.bin`
(`tools/bake_terminal_lakes_1318.py`): lakes.bin with the Aral at 29 m on the 2′ grid (430 cells, 4,174 km², the cells
below 29 m joined to the western basin; its green shore 15 km instead of 40), its other patches dropped so the grid's own
cells come back (the dry bed, the North Aral as today), every other lake's patches and record byte for byte. It gives the
river ends their lake (the Amu Darya and the Syr Darya end in the Aral of 1318) and the scales without outline coasts the
same Aral. `UpgradeExistingWorldRiverEnds` still gives rules 2: an upgrade never moves a world's Aral.

Both files ship in the mod's zip (`EarthMap.csproj`'s earthdata list; no upload; the zip 35.5 MB). A world of these rules on a build without works-1318-4.bin warns at start as for any works file (EarthMapSystem.Data).

Every area here, and what is left as it is below, has its entry in the ledger of human works undone
([`docs/works-1318-ledger.md`](../works-1318-ledger.md), tables C and D).

## Not done, and why

- **The Bitter Lakes**: dry since channel rules 1 (2.0.0). Their salt basin's depth (no open source for the floor) and
  a salt-crust surface (no surface rule) are left.
- **Tulare Lake**: 0005 revives it at 61 m (1,890 km² in an rc.13 world, the 1870s lake). The sources put the lake of
  about 1300 near a medieval low below 61 m, but no level or extent for it: 0005's lake stays.
- **The North Aral** stays as the grid has it today (41 m, the Kokaral dam of 2005); the Small Aral of 1318 was low, but
  its bed under today's water is unknown.
- **The other Fens meres** (Ugg, Ramsey, Benwick, Trundle, Brick, Soham) have no open outline; tracing them from the
  public-domain maps is later work. Whittlesey Mere is OSM's (its 1851 remnant, 4 km²; 7.6–12 km² earlier).
- **The rest of the worldwide list** (Tule and Lower Klamath, La Janda, Kolindsund, Martin Mere, Bientina, Comacchio,
  Dongting, ...), Mareotis and Abu Qir, Sarykamysh, the river re-routings (the Aare, Linth, Kander, the Syr Darya's
  Kerderi braid, the Amu Darya to the Sarykamysh): no quick open outline or a level, or a course edit; later.
- **Depths**: a LAKE area of 1318 takes the outline lakes' old bed (0.05 × the distance to the shore, up to 40 m, at least
  3 blocks a few blocks out); lake rules 1's real depths reach the water mask's lakes only.
