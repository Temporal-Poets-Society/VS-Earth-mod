# 0046: Calm rivers: white water only at the drops of steep rivers, flowing ordinary water elsewhere

Date: 2 November 2026. Status: implemented on `claude/calm-rivers` (from `release/1.0` `34c6c54`) in Earth, Hydrology and Sea, playtest versions Earth 1.0.3-rivers.3, Hydrology 0.6.2-rivers.7 (rivers.2 to .6: filling enclosed pockets, from the author's playtests at the Nile; rivers.7: no white specks on calm river water), Sea 0.5.3-rivers.1. Offline, headless-server and graphical-client verified (below; boat drift not measured). Nothing committed, packaged for release, installed or uploaded. New worlds only.

## Report

A player (Dskater84, Discord, 2 November), on the Olt near Racovița (45.5637° N 24.2618° E; standing at 45.6955° N 24.2755° E):

- placing a block in the river turns the two blocks around it into still water;
- the water runs so fast that boats are dragged away, and it is loud;
- the river has "almost no level difference"; they would prefer Watersheds' slowly running water.

The author adds the Tanaro (45.0277° N 8.8445° E), where rapid water ends abruptly in completely still water, and that Sea's waves move vanilla rapid water. The author's reply to the player: rapids should be where the real river has a rapid elevation change; ordinary flowing water had problems before.

## Cause

- **Too many rapids reaches.** Pool layouts 8–10 make a reach rapids when its real valley falls 0.5 m/km or more (`RapidsSlope`), whatever the river's size. At Earth size 1,024,000, 38.4 % of river length is rapids, including the Olt at Racovița (0.56 m/km), the Aare at Bern, the Inn and the Snake. Vanilla's rapid water pushes 0.004 per tick (ordinary water 0.0015) and plays the loud "rapids" ambience.
- **Still water between the rapids.** Calm reaches were drawn as still sources (`water-still-7`). Where a rapids reach ended, the flowing surface stopped at a line.
- **Relabelling.** Vanilla's `OnDelayedWaterUpdateCheck` recomputes the flow label of the changed cell and its eight neighbours. Hydrology's query v2 kept labels only on rapids sources, so in calm reaches a block change could turn the cells around it still (the player's first point). In headless runs, a temporary poke at the Tanaro changed four labels from still to flowing with v2; the player's still halo did not reproduce exactly.
- **Sea's waves on rivers.** Sea replaces vanilla's liquid warping with its swell, with the amplitude divided by 5 × max(0.2, 1 − oceanity) and never zero. Inland, where Earth's ocean map is 0, river water still had a fifth of the open sea's divisor in strength.

## Decision

Agreed by the author on 2 November ("Yes, build it"):

1. **Rapids by stream power (Earth pool layout 11).** A reach is rapids when its valley slope (m/m) × √Q (m³/s) is at least 0.025 (`RapidsPower`), with a valley fall of at least 0.3 m/km (`RapidsMinSlope`). This is a proxy for stream power (slope × discharge), with the square root chosen so that large lowland rivers do not all qualify. Large rivers in real gorges keep their rapids (the Danube's Iron Gates at 0.43 m/km and 5,505 m³/s); middle-sized rivers on gentle valleys become calm.
2. **Calm rivers flow.** The top source of a calm river column is ordinary water labelled with the river's flow (`water-<dir>-7`), not still water. Boats drift slowly with it (vanilla's ordinary push), and it plays the quiet creek sound.
3. **White water only at the drops** (the author, 3 November, replacing the first build's fade below). Rapid water is no longer the whole surface of a rapids reach. It appears only at the reach's drops: each step's edge (rapid, as before) and the ramp below it, with the top source under the ramp. The pools between the steps are calm flowing water like every other river. At the Congo about 7 % of a rapids reach's columns carry a ramp, so each step shows a band of white water a few blocks long across the river. Earth's query (`RiverAt`) reports rapids only at those columns: a ramp column, or a step's edge (a ramp beside it on the four sides, one block lower, as `SettleSteps` finds edges), so Hydrology keeps the pools calm when blocks change.
   - The first build (1.0.3-rivers.1) mixed rapids and calm reaches over 24 blocks × SizeScale where they met, by a column hash in 3 × 3 patches. In the client that read as a patchwork of squares; with white water only at drops there is nothing left to mix, so the fade is removed.
   - The author first asked for rapids to be dropped entirely ("Off" as the default, option 1). Measured first: with "Off", the Congo's Livingstone rapids keep no rapid water at all, because Earth's 1-block steps are far apart and almost never form the 2-block drop Hydrology needs; only Niagara and Victoria Falls kept any, from their waterfalls. So "Off" stays an option, not the default.
4. **A world option.** `earthRiverRapids` (Earth tab, creation only): "At the drops of steep rivers" (`steep`, default) or "Off" (`EarthParams.NoRiverRapids`, proto 90). "Off" makes every reach calm: no white water on river steps, waterfalls stay. Old saves read false and keep their rapids.
5. **Hydrology keeps every river column's label (query v3).** Earth reports `RiverQueryVersion` 3 for layout 11. Hydrology's `EarthRiverQuery.LabelledFlow` returns the flow for every river column (v3) or only for rapids (v2). `H4Rapids.RiverFlowLabel` uses it, so vanilla's relabelling keeps calm water flowing. Hydrology reports `EarthRiverQueryVersion` 3, and Earth gives new worlds layout 11 only when it sees 3 (else 10 with 2, 8 with 1, 7 without). Hydrology's foam throttle (`C1RapidFoam`) also covers labelled calm sources. It was a 0.2 share (`CalmSourceRipples`) up to 0.6.2-rivers.6. From rivers.7 it is none (`CalmSourceSpecks` = 0): the author saw vanilla's white specks on slow water as bubbles (3 November). A calm river's surface now throws no specks, like a lake's still water, and its texture still shows the current. Flowing water at ramps, edges and falls keeps vanilla's specks.
6. **No swell on inland water (Sea).** `seaWaves` fades Sea's swell in only where the divisor is below 5 (oceanity above 0); inland water uses vanilla's own liquid warping. `MeshWaveFactorAt` and the celestial differential use the same fade, so inland its factor is 0.
7. **New worlds only.** A world keeps the pool layout saved at creation. The player's world keeps its rapids and still water; the fix needs a new world (or a new Earth world in the same game).

Not done: lowering vanilla's push or sound on Earth's rapids, per-river animation speeds (vanilla has only ordinary and rapid water; varying speed would need Sea's water shader and a speed per column from Earth: a separate proposal), and changing worlds already created.

With the change, the labels beside placed blocks stay flowing. The Olt site found no river middle at the exact coordinate (the harness's ±6 × ±4 search).

  - Headless servers (Congo, Iron Gates, Olt, Tanaro, a Congo `river_block`). rivers.2's query (ramp columns only) let Hydrology turn 11 rapid step edges at the Iron Gates into ordinary water on the first update; with edges beside a ramp on all eight sides, 8 calm cells turned rapid. rivers.3 (the four sides, the ramp one block lower) changes no cell's water kind at any site on the first update or after pokes. - **The author's Nile playtest** (3 November, Earth 1.0.3-rivers.3 with Hydrology 0.6.2-rivers.1, the author's own world at Cairo). The river looked right. A basin dug beside the river filled, except at the corner where it overlapped the river's columns. There a 5 × 7 patch of rapid, partly filled water stayed for good, with two cells 1–2 blocks low. A copy of the save, loaded headless, showed the cause in Hydrology. In natural river columns Hydrology refuses to fill water resting on water: the guard that keeps Earth's sill-less steps from flooding. A pocket of flowing water 3 deep stayed in the river's own pool, and its drop kept the bank's sources rapid edges. Hydrology 0.6.2-rivers.3 fills such an enclosed pocket (`RiverPockets`, since rivers.6 `FillPockets`; Hydrology `docs/design.md`). A cell at the rim of an old pocket also wakes the cell below it, so a block placed and broken there reaches the layer that can fill.
  - On the save copy: the old build left the pocket unchanged after every cell was woken (38 refusals). The new build cleared it after a single block was placed and broken (no rapid water, no holes; 17 pocket fills).
  - Regression, headless (rivers.3): the river scenarios above (Congo, Iron Gates, Olt, Tanaro, Congo `river_block`) show no new water changes. A placed-and-broken block at the Iron Gates and the Tanaro no longer leaves a flowing cell behind (the 2 pocket fills). The step-break scenarios (`scenarios-sill-break.json`: sill, ramp bed, bank and mid-bed at Congo, Rhine and Daugava steps) made 0 pocket fills. The sill and ramp-bed results are identical to the old build's. The bank and mid-bed sites were picked at different spots in the two runs, so they are not compared.
- **The author's delta pit** (3 November, Earth 1.0.3-rivers.3 with Hydrology 0.6.2-rivers.3, a new world). The author reported "better but still 3 cells left". A pit dug into the river bed in the delta, 15 blocks deep, filled, except for a nook a block wide and 10 to 14 deep beside the river, outside the river's columns. Three columns of rapid flowing water stayed there, fed by rapid sources beside them. Hydrology's ground rule keeps a 1-wide channel flowing, and its vessels case needs a 2 × 2 square. Hydrology 0.6.2-rivers.6 makes the pocket fill general wherever water flooding is on (`FillPockets`). A pocket's cells may rest on a source, or hold water and rest on the ground. So with flooding on, a 1-wide ditch or nook enclosed by water now fills, where it used to carry 6 blocks of flowing water.
  - A first try (rivers.5) also let dry cells on the ground count, and Hydrology's offline river test then flooded its steps upward. That test's last pool has no outflow, and its flush banks, closed in by the test world's walls, became part of a "pocket". In rivers.6 a dry cell on the ground is land and ends a pocket; the offline suite passes again (14,315 / 14,315).
  - On copies of both of the author's saves, after a single block was placed and broken: the delta nook and the Nile pocket leave no rapid water, no flowing water and no holes. Waking the whole area gives the same result. The installed rivers.3 left the delta nook unchanged even with every cell woken.
  - Regression, headless (rivers.6): the river scenarios match rivers.3 (2 pocket fills, both at a placed-and-broken block). The step-break scenarios were placed at the same spots as the old build's this time, and all twelve match it: 0 pocket fills, and no raised sources. The one extra cell is water filling a dry cell 2 below the surface beside a broken bank block, with no Hydrology fill in the whole run (0), so it is vanilla's own spreading.
